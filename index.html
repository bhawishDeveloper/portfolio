About Me

I'm Bhawish Raj, a Data Engineer and Business Analyst with 3+ years of experience spanning banking, mobility, and early-stage tech. At UBL, Pakistan's largest bank, I led a 5-person BI team and automated reporting pipelines processing 5TB+ daily, cutting manual reporting effort by 70%.
