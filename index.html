I'm Bhawish Raj, a Data Engineer and Business Analyst with 3+ years of experience spanning banking, mobility, and early-stage tech. At UBL, Pakistan's largest bank, I led a 5-person BI team and automated reporting pipelines processing 5TB+ daily, cutting manual reporting effort by 70%. At Careem and Desider Labs I built ETL flows, dashboards, and ML models, including an election-forecasting model with 92% accuracy, that turned messy survey and operational data into decisions stakeholders could act on.

My toolkit includes Python, SQL, Airflow, Spark, Kafka, Power BI, and Tableau across the AWS/GCP/Azure cloud stack. I'm now pursuing an MSc in Data Science at Åbo Akademi University in Finland, focused on large-scale data systems and statistical modeling, while working part-time as a Data Analyst at Since AI.
